Understanding the Results

Accuracy measures the overall proportion of correct predictions. Precision measures how many predicted defaults were actually defaults. Recall measures how many actual defaults were identified.

F1 Score balances precision and recall. ROC-AUC measures the model's ability to distinguish between the two classes, while PR-AUC focuses on precision and recall.

These results are based on the project's test dataset. They are educational model evaluations and should not be used as real-world credit approval decisions.
